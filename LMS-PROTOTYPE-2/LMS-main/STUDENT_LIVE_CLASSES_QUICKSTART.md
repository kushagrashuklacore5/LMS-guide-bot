# Student Live Classes - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Quick Summary

Students can now see and join live classes from their enrolled courses directly in the Student Dashboard. No more external links—everything happens within the portal with embedded Jitsi meetings.

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Mentors (Creating Live Classes)

### Step 1: Go to Course Portal
- Login as mentor
- Select a course

### Step 2: Schedule a Live Class
- Click "Schedule Live Class" button
- Fill in:
  - **Title:** Class name (e.g., "Mathematics Lecture")
  - **Description:** Brief description
  - **Start Time:** When the class starts
  - **End Time:** When the class ends
- Click "Schedule"

### Step 3: Confirm
- Meeting ID and link are **auto-generated**
- Class appears in your "Scheduled Classes" list

### Step 4: When Ready to Start
- Click "Start Live Class" button
- Jitsi meeting opens in the portal
- You'll be the **moderator** (can mute/control)

### Step 5: End the Class
- Close the meeting when done
- Optionally click the delete button (×) to remove the class

---

## 👨‍🎓 For Students (Joining Live Classes)

### Step 1: Open Student Dashboard
- Login as student
- You'll be on the Dashboard by default

### Step 2: Look for "Upcoming Live Classes" Section
- Scroll down on the dashboard
- You'll see a section with 🎥 icon titled "🎥 Upcoming Live Classes"
- Only shows classes from **your enrolled courses**

### Step 3: Review Class Details
Each class card shows:
- **Class Title**
- **Description**
- **Scheduled Start Time**
- **Duration**
- **Status Badge:**
  - 🔴 "LIVE NOW" (pulsing red) = Class is happening now
  - Blue badge = "Scheduled" for future
  - Gray badge = "Completed" for past

### Step 4: Join the Class
- Click the **"Join Now"** (if live) or **"Join Class"** (if scheduled) button
- Wait a moment while your attendance is recorded
- Jitsi meeting opens in a modal window within the portal

### Step 5: Participate
- You can:
  - ✅ See the instructor's video and screen share
  - ✅ Hear the audio
  - ✅ Ask questions in chat
  - ✅ Share your video/audio (if enabled)
- You **cannot:**
  - ❌ Mute other participants
  - ❌ Control the recording
  - ❌ Change settings (instructor only)

### Step 6: Exit the Class
- Click the **"X" button** in the top-right of the Jitsi modal
- This closes the modal but doesn't end the meeting for others
- You can rejoin if needed

## 🔐 Security & Permissions

### Students Can ONLY See:
- Live classes from courses they are **enrolled in**
- Cannot see other students' classes
- Cannot access classes from courses they haven't joined

### Students CANNOT Do:
- Modify class details
- Delete classes
- Control who joins
- Mute/unmute others
- End the meeting (instructor only)

### Attendance is Automatically Recorded:
- When you join, your attendance is logged
- Join time is stored in the database
- Instructors can see who attended

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I see live classes before they're scheduled?
**A:** No, only scheduled and live classes appear. Completed classes may remain visible depending on settings.

### Q: What if I don't see the "Upcoming Live Classes" section?
**A:** 
- You may not be enrolled in any courses with scheduled classes
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Check if you're logged in correctly

### Q: Can I join a class that already started?
**A:** Yes! As long as the instructor hasn't ended the meeting, you can join at any time.

### Q: What happens if the instructor ends the meeting?
**A:** You'll be disconnected. If you try to rejoin, you'll get a message that the room is closed.

### Q: Can I record the class on my own?
**A:** That depends on your browser permissions and Jitsi settings. The instructor can enable/disable recording.

### Q: What if my internet is slow?
**A:** Jitsi will adjust video quality automatically. You can also disable video and just use audio.

### Q: Can I leave and come back?
**A:** Yes! The close button (X) just closes the modal. You can rejoin by clicking the Join button again (if the class is still active).

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### "No live classes showing"
1. Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
2. Check if you're enrolled in courses
3. Ask your instructor to create a live class
4. Check browser console for errors (F12)

### "Can't join the meeting"
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure JavaScript is enabled in browser
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
4. Check if the class is still active

### "Camera/Microphone not working"
1. Grant permissions when Jitsi asks
2. Check browser settings (Settings → Privacy → Camera/Microphone)
3. Ensure no other app is using the camera
4. Test your camera in system settings

### "Can't see instructor's video"
1. Check their internet connection
2. Ask them to share their screen instead
3. Refresh the page
4. Try a different browser

### "Chat not visible"
- Look for the **chat icon** in Jitsi interface
- Click to expand the chat panel
- Scroll up to see previous messages

## 📊 Attendance Tracking

### How It Works:
- When you click "Join Class", your attendance is recorded immediately
- Your name is sent to the instructor's dashboard
- If you leave and rejoin, both times are logged
- Instructors can see attendance reports

### Privacy:
- Only your instructor can see who attended
- Attendance is tied to your student account
- Cannot be deleted or modified once recorded

## 🎓 Best Practices

### Before Class:
- ✅ Test your camera and microphone
- ✅ Find a quiet location
- ✅ Join a few minutes early
- ✅ Close other tabs to improve performance

### During Class:
- ✅ Mute yourself when not speaking (unless instructor says otherwise)
- ✅ Keep your camera on (instructor may require this)
- ✅ Take notes in your notebook or text editor
- ✅ Ask questions in chat or unmute to speak

### After Class:
- ✅ Review any shared materials
- ✅ Download any resources the instructor provided
- ✅ Check if recording is available for rewatch
